# Future plans

Ideas left out of phase 1 on purpose, roughly in the order I'd pick them up.

1. **Second-order journey for new customers.** Average month-1 retention is 21.2%: most new customers don't order
   again the next month. A short onboarding sequence, tested with a control group the same way, is the natural
   phase 2.
2. **Look after Champions.** 761 customers (13% of identified customers) bring in 46.6% of sales. Losing a few of
   them would hurt more than anything this phase fixes, so they need priority service, not discounts.
3. **Cheap test for Lost customers.** 2,052 customers who ordered about 1.5 times each. A single no-discount
   reminder, with a control group, would show whether they're worth any spend.
4. **Repeat the win-back monthly** and pool the control groups for a more certain uplift figure.
5. **Returns.** 7.34% of sales value comes back. Find which products and countries drive it.
6. **Rebuild the dashboard in Power BI or Tableau** from the same SQL outputs.
